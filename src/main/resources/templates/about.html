🩺 Clinical Standards & Healthcare Mission

NutriCheck is an integrated digital clinical platform designed to bridge preventive health analytics, chronic disease risk monitoring, and personalized dietary regimens. We empower patients and healthcare professionals to collaborate seamlessly on lifestyle and metabolic health.

🎯 Our Clinical Mission

Metabolic health disorders, cardiovascular risks, and weight fluctuations require accurate, continuous biometric tracking and targeted dietary intervention. NutriCheck eliminates the guesswork by combining continuous vital monitoring with rule-matched medical nutrition therapy and certified clinical consultations.

🔬 Core Medical & Technical Pillars

📊 WHO-Standard BMI Analytics

Automated metric classification into clinical tiers (Underweight, Normal, Overweight, Obese) with longitudinal trend modeling.

🥗 Medical Nutrition Matching

Evidence-based dietary protocols with structured macronutrient breakdowns tailored to individual biometric parameters.

🩺 Biometric Vitals Tracking

Centralized logging for Blood Pressure (systolic/diastolic), Fasting Blood Glucose, and Total Cholesterol profiles.

🔒 Multi-Tier Privacy & Security

Cryptographic password hashing, strict role-based data isolation, and protected clinical consultation scheduling.
